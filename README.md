# Bid Master – Online Auction Management System

## Overview
Bid Master is an advanced platform that streamlines online auctions from item listing to delivery, ensuring transparency, security, and a seamless experience for every stakeholder — from seller to buyer.

---

**Project Title:** Bid Master — Auction Management Platform  
**Role:** Full-Stack Developer | System Designer  
**Tech Stack:** MERN Stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js) | Stripe

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🔨 Item Listing & Seller Dashboard**  
  Sellers can submit items for bidding through an intuitive dashboard.

- **🔍 Item Authentication**  
  All submitted items are thoroughly reviewed and authenticated by an Inspection Panel prior to auction listing.

- **⏱️ Timed Bidding System**  
  Approved items are auctioned within a defined time window, ensuring fair and competitive bidding.
  - **Email Notifications:** Bidders receive emails when they are outbid and when they win an auction, keeping everyone informed in real time.

- **💳 Secure Payments**  
  Integrated Stripe for fast and safe transactions.

- **👥 HR Management**  
  Manage auctioneers, finance, and IT roles. Assign tasks and monitor progress.

- **🚚 Shipping Management**  
  Assign couriers and track item delivery post-sale for a complete end-to-end solution.

---

## Why Bid Master?
- **Transparency:** Every step, from item submission to delivery, is tracked and auditable.
- **Security:** Robust authentication and secure payment processing.
- **Efficiency:** Streamlined workflows for sellers, buyers, and administrators.
